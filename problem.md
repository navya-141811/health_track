Affordable Smart Health Monitoring and Emergency Alert System

Problem Statement

Elderly people and individuals requiring regular health monitoring may not always receive timely attention when their measured health parameters become abnormal or when they need emergency assistance.

Develop an affordable health-monitoring prototype that collects or simulates vital-sign measurements, such as heart rate and body temperature, displays the readings in real time, and generates alerts when values cross configurable thresholds. The system should provide a simple interface for caregivers to view readings and acknowledge alerts. It must clearly distinguish prototype alerts from medical diagnosis.

Expected deliverable: A working hardware or software prototype with live or simulated readings, configurable threshold alerts, a caregiver dashboard, and an emergency notification demonstration.

Suggested technologies: ESP32, suitable sensors or simulated data, Python, loT protocols, and a web/mobile application.
